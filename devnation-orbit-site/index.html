01 / What lives in Orbit

A club is more
than a feed.

Orbit brings the things DevNation members make and do into one place.

Events you can actually join.

Browse approved, published events. Registration respects audience, deadlines and available seats; organisers can review attendance and feedback.

Projects with a story.

Members submit their builds and track milestones. Approved submissions and approved GitHub-org imports appear in the public showcase.

People behind the work.

Explore the public member directory and a leaderboard built from coding activity and attended events.

02 / How a member finds their feet

Come curious.
Leave connected.

  1. Explore freely.

    Events, approved projects, members and leaderboard standings are browsable without signing in.

  2. Join with your GitHub identity.

    Sign-in is for allowlisted people. Onboarding adds profile details; admins review membership and manage role-based access.

  3. Make something together.

    Approved members can propose events and submit projects for review. Event eligibility depends on the audience; approved member registrations and attendance feed the event score.

The public site remains open. Admin tools are for authorised club roles.

04 / Under the hood

Made by builders.
For builders.

Orbit is a real club platform, not a score painted on a poster. Its event and project data, member profiles and calculated standings live behind a full-stack application.

A board that moves with the work.

Page visits drain small batches of stale GitHub and LeetCode stats. A GitHub webhook can put new activity first in the queue; scores are recalculated when data changes.

Moderation where it matters.

Admins manage approval, visibility and scoring weights. Public project and event listings only include reviewed content.
